# Third-Party and Cloud Outsourcing Register

## 1 Material outsourcing arrangements

| Provider | Service | Material | Data location | Exit plan last tested |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stratus Cloud | Hosting of online and mobile banking | Yes | Hong Kong, Singapore | April 2026 |
| Ledgerline | Card processing | Yes | Hong Kong | Not yet tested |
| Brightdesk | Customer contact CRM | No | Singapore | n/a |

## 2 Governance

2.1 Before entering a new material outsourcing arrangement, the bank consults its supervisor
and obtains approval from the Board Risk Committee.

2.2 Each material provider undergoes an annual due diligence review covering financial
condition, security controls and sub-contracting.

2.3 Every material arrangement has a documented exit plan, tested at least every two years.
The Ledgerline exit plan test is scheduled for **Q1 2027**.

## 3 Audit and access rights

3.1 All material contracts give the bank, its auditors and its supervisor the right to access
and audit the provider.
