# Access Management Standard

## 1 Principles

1.1 Access is granted on a least-privilege and need-to-know basis.

## 2 Joiners, movers and leavers

2.1 Access for a leaver is revoked within **1 business day** of their last working day.
2.2 A mover's previous access is removed within **5 business days** of the move.

## 3 Privileged access

3.1 Privileged accounts are reviewed **every quarter** by the system owner.
3.2 Privileged sessions are recorded and the recordings retained for **1 year**.

## 4 Authentication

4.1 Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access and for all privileged
access.
